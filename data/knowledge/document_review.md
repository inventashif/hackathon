# Document review and escalation

Documents added by an operator remain pending and are excluded from retrieval until a designated reviewer checks the source, revision, plant applicability, and approval status. Keep the original filename and retain the document revision so reviewers can trace the source.

Escalate any safety-critical question, conflicting source, missing citation, low-evidence result, or out-of-scope request to the responsible supervisor or engineering authority. Stop work and follow the site's established escalation process while waiting for qualified guidance.

Query analytics record whether the system found evidence, the retrieval relevance score, and the time of the request. They are intended for evaluating the demo workflow, not measuring plant safety or productivity.
